# ShopKart Return & Refund Policy (v3.2)

## P1 - Return window
Customers may request a return or refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are not eligible and must be rejected.

## P2 - Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in original condition with tags and packaging. Electronics or home appliances that have been opened or used but are in full working condition can be returned within the window for a partial refund of 85% of the price paid (a 15% restocking fee applies).

## P3 - Non-returnable categories
Innerwear, perishables, groceries, gift cards and personalised or customised items cannot be returned or refunded unless they arrived damaged. Requests for these categories must be rejected.

## P4 - Damaged or defective on arrival
If an item arrives damaged or is defective, the customer is entitled to a full refund provided the issue is reported within 7 days of delivery and a photo of the damage or defect is supplied. Without a photo, ask the customer for photo proof before deciding.

## P5 - Wrong item delivered
If the customer received a different item than ordered, a full refund is issued once photo proof of the received item is supplied. Without a photo, ask the customer for photo proof before deciding.

## P6 - Missing parts or accessories
If an item arrives with a part or accessory missing, the customer receives a partial refund equal to the listed value of the missing component, provided it is reported within 7 days of delivery.

## P7 - Non-delivery
A refund for non-delivery is issued only when the courier confirms the package as lost, or when it has not been delivered 10 days after the expected delivery date. Orders still in transit before that point are not yet eligible and the request must be rejected with an explanation.

## P8 - Serial number verification
For electronics, the serial number supplied by the customer must match the serial number on record for the order. A mismatch must be held for fraud review by a human agent.

## P9 - Automatic refund limit
Refunds of Rs 2,000 or less may be processed automatically. Any refund above Rs 2,000 requires approval by a human agent before it is paid.

## P10 - Repeat returners
Customers with 3 or more refund requests in the previous 60 days are flagged for possible refund abuse. Any refund for a flagged customer must be reviewed by a human agent.

## P11 - Refund amount cap
A refund can never exceed the amount the customer actually paid for the item.

## P12 - Change of mind
Customers may return unused items for a change of mind, including wrong size or colour, within the 30-day window for a full refund, provided P2 and P3 are satisfied.
